Award Categories

Ten award categories will be presented in this convocation.

01

Best Overall Student Award

Honours the student with the most outstanding all-round academic and co-curricular performance.

02

Academic Excellence Award

Recognises exceptional academic achievement and outstanding cumulative results.

03

Dean's List Award

Awarded for consistent high academic performance across multiple semesters.

04

Best Program Graduate

Presented to the top graduate of each programme for excellence in their field of study.

05

Outstanding Leadership Award

Recognises students who have demonstrated exceptional leadership in campus organisations and initiatives.

06

Extracurricular Achievement Award

Celebrates notable contributions and achievements in clubs, societies, and community activities.

07

Innovation & Research Award

Honours innovative projects, research breakthroughs, and creative problem-solving.

08

Best Final Year Project Award

Rewards the most outstanding final year project in terms of quality, impact, and originality.

09

Sportsman & Sportswoman of the Year

Recognises the top male and female athletes for sporting excellence and sportsmanship.

10

Special Recognition Award

Given for exceptional contributions, perseverance, or distinguished service beyond regular categories.
