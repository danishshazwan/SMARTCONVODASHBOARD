Who We Are

About Us

SmartConvo is an AI-assisted convocation queue and identity verification system built on face recognition technology, designed to deliver a smooth, secure and efficient graduation experience.

Real-Time Queue Management

Automated queue tracking keeps students, staff and guests informed of current status at every stage of the convocation flow.

Face Identity Verification

Face recognition confirms each attendee's identity securely, minimising manual checks and reducing verification time.

Secure & Reliable

Role-based access for guests, students and admins ensures the right people see the right information at the right time.

OUR MISSION

Making convocation day effortless

We combine intelligent queue orchestration with face-based identity verification so that every graduate, guest and administrator can navigate the ceremony with confidence, speed and peace of mind.
